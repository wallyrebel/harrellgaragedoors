Garage Door Questions

Do you work on residential and commercial garage doors?

Yes. Harrell Garage Doors installs and services residential doors and commercial overhead doors. Call with your door type, size and the work you need.

Can you repair a broken garage door spring or cable?

We service springs, cables and other door hardware. If a spring has broken or a cable is loose, stop operating the door and keep people clear. Springs and cables are under tension and should be handled by a trained technician.

Should I repair my garage door or replace it?

That depends on the condition of the panels, tracks, springs and opener. An isolated worn part may be repairable; extensive damage or recurring problems may make replacement worth considering. We can discuss the options after assessing your door.

How do I request an estimate in Tippah County?

Call (662) 223-0748 or email dell.harrell@yahoo.com. Include your town, phone number, door size if known, and a description of the problem or installation project. Photos can help explain the work.

How soon can you come out?

Call for current availability. Scheduling depends on your location, the work needed and parts availability. If a door is stuck open or the property cannot be secured, mention that when you call.
