Garage Door Opener Repair & Installation in North MS

When a remote stops working or a door keeps reversing, the opener is only one possible cause. Harrell Garage Doors troubleshoots garage door systems and installs openers for customers in Tippah County and the wider North Mississippi area.

Common opener problems

  • The remote or wall control does not operate the door
  • The opener runs but the door does not move
  • The door stops partway or reverses when closing
  • Safety sensors indicate a problem
  • The opener is noisy or works intermittently

Make a note of the opener brand, model number and any flashing lights if they are safely visible. Let us know whether the problem happens with the remote, wall control or both.

The door itself matters

An opener cannot compensate for a damaged or unbalanced door. Broken springs, damaged cables and track problems can make an opener appear faulty. If the door is crooked, unusually heavy or has damaged hardware, stop using it and ask about garage door repair.

Do not bypass safety sensors or hold down controls to force a damaged door to operate. Keep the opening clear while waiting for service.

Choosing a replacement opener

Discuss your door size, available ceiling space, noise preferences and any features you want, such as additional remotes, a keypad or connected controls. Compatibility and feature availability depend on the door and opener model. We can help you compare appropriate options for the installation.

Set up service

Call with your location and a description of the issue, or email photos of the opener label and door. If you are planning a complete replacement, we can discuss the opener alongside your new garage door.
