Tippah County: Ripley and our Tiplersville base

Harrell Garage Doors is based in Tiplersville and travels to customers in Ripley, Walnut, Falkner, Blue Mountain and surrounding Tippah County communities. For a repair, tell us whether the door is stuck open or closed. For an installation, let us know whether you are replacing a door or finishing a new opening.

Alcorn County: Corinth

We provide garage door repair, installation and opener service for homes and businesses in Corinth and the surrounding Alcorn County area. When arranging a visit outside the city, include your street or road name so we can confirm the service location and access.

Prentiss County: Booneville

Customers in Booneville and Prentiss County can contact us for spring and cable repairs, replacement garage doors and opener troubleshooting. Share the door type and any visible damage when you request service; parts and scheduling depend on the system involved.

Union County: New Albany

We travel to New Albany and Union County for residential and commercial garage door work. If you are planning an installation, include the approximate opening size and whether there is an existing opener. We can discuss the next steps for measuring and estimating the project.

Lafayette County: Oxford

Our North Mississippi service area includes Oxford and Lafayette County. Contact us for a door repair, opener service or a replacement estimate. If another person will provide access to the property, coordinate the location and availability when scheduling.

DeSoto County: Southaven

We also serve Southaven and the wider DeSoto County area. Call with your property address, door problem or installation plans to confirm scheduling. For commercial work, describe any vehicle clearance or business access requirements before the visit.

Nearby communities and out-of-state requests
